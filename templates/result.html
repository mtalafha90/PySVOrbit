❔ How do I tell if this is a good fit?
Look at reduced chi² per data type below: values close to 1 mean the fit matches your data about as well as your quoted measurement errors would predict — a good sign. Much larger than 1 usually means the model doesn't quite match the data (or your errors were underestimated); much smaller than 1 usually means the errors were overestimated. Also check RMS (root-mean-square residual): it tells you, in real units, how far the fit typically misses each data point.
